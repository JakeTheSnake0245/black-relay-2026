Black Relay site kit · field card

CWWK site box · keep with the kit
✓ ANY CABLE, ANY PORT
Modems (ISP router, Starlink router, the Spitz's LAN port), a laptop or a switch: plug each into any free port on the CWWK.
Within about a minute the CWWK works out what each one is: modems join the secure link, the Spitz brings the Wi-Fi, laptops and switches join the site network.
✗ ONE CABLE PER DEVICE
Never plug the same box into two ports.
Moved a cable? Fine. Wait one minute and it carries on by itself.

Power up

  1. Modems first: ISP router; Starlink (open sky, up to 5 min); Spitz (needs a SIM for its own cellular).
  2. Then the CWWK. Ready about 2 min after power-on; starts everything itself.
  3. Wait for green on the controller's Health page.
  4. Get on the network: Wi-Fi GL-X3000-0fd (or -5G), or plug a laptop into a free port.
  5. Controller → Setup → Run self-test. Fix the first red line first.

What the colours mean

  • Green: working.
  • Amber: working, but look at it (e.g. one modem down: slower).
  • Red: down. Follow the "Fix it" box on Health.
  • Grey port: nothing plugged in there (fine).

Golden rules

  • Wait 60 s after any fix; it heals itself.
  • Nothing leaks: if the secure link is down, nothing is sent at all.
  • Last resort: reboot the CWWK (power button). Back in about 2 min.
  • Shade and airflow: the CWWK has no fan.
  • Safe to press: self-test, blink port, restart tunnel, start a container.

Which jack is which?

Controller → Setup → Blink port flashes that jack's light for 15 s. The Setup page shows what is detected on every port.

Top fixes

You seeDo this
No internet at allIs at least one modem plugged in with its power on? Setup shows each modem found. Modems found but no lanes: the modems have no internet, or the hub is down: call the hub admin.
One modem red / slowThe others carry the traffic. Power-cycle that modem (unplug 10 s). Starlink: clear sky, wait 5 min, check it via its quick link or the Starlink app.
No Wi-FiThe Spitz must be on, with its LAN port cabled to the CWWK (any port). Give it 2 min after power-on.
Laptop on a cable gets no addressWait one minute. Still nothing after 2 min: unplug it for 5 s and plug it back in.
Can't open the controllerBe on the site Wi-Fi or a laptop port, and try https://10.40.0.10. Off site: https://headscale.l8s.dev/cwwk/ (needs the tunnel up). Still nothing: reboot the CWWK.
"Leak guard NOT armed" or "CWWK is hot"Leak guard: stop sensitive traffic, reboot the CWWK, call the kit owner if it stays red. Hot: shade it, clear its vents, nothing stacked on top.
